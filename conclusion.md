# Conclusion

This prototype project outlines the framework for analyzing multiple types of
extreme weather events, including tornado outbreaks, heatwaves, and lake-effect
snowstorms. By organizing the project into sections such as event overviews,
data sources, methodology, and visualization examples.

Although the prototype does not yet include full analysis, it demonstrates how
ERA5 reanalysis, GFS model output, satellite imagery, and surface/station
observations will be incorporated into the final study. The notebooks and
workflow outline how time series, spatial plots, and model-verification
techniques can be applied consistently across different cases of extreme
weather.

This prototype serves as the foundation for the upcoming group project, where we
will expand each section, add full datasets, and complete detailed evaluations
of model performance.
